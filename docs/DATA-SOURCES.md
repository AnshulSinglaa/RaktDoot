# Data sources: what is real, what is synthetic, and why

Checked on 2026-10-20. Rule: real where real open data exists, synthetic (and labelled) where it does not or where
using it would be wrong (donors are private people).

## The problem in numbers (for the problem statement)
- India's stated annual requirement is 14.6 million units; 14.6 million were collected in 2024-25, but the requirement
  figure comes from a 2018 NBTC study on a 2017 population, so the real need is likely higher
  ([Newslaundry / IndiaSpend, 2026](https://www.newslaundry.com/2026/03/02/govt-claims-it-is-blood-sufficient-but-is-it)).
  Independent estimates put the gap near 1 million units a year
  ([ISBT Transfusion Today, 2025](https://isbtweb.foleon.com/transfusion-today/transfusion-today-july-2025-copy-1/unmet-needs)).
- The averages hide the real problem, which is distribution: a 2024 geospatial study of eight northern states
  (660 million people, including MP) found only about 61% of people within an hour of a blood-equipped facility, and
  defined "blood deserts" as places more than 30 minutes' drive from a blood bank
  ([BMJ Global Health 2024, PMC11499795](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11499795/)).
  That is exactly what Rakt Doot attacks: not "more blood", but "the right unit, at the nearest bank that really has
  it, confirmed, in minutes".

## Used in the app
| Data | Source | Licence | Real? | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MP blood banks (119 in the snapshot, 118 imported) and hospitals (2,104, 1,929 imported): names, locations, public phones, ownership | OpenStreetMap, largely the 2017 Ministry of Health directory import (`Backend/data/osm/`), loaded by `manage.py import-osm` | ODbL 1.0, attribution required | Real, possibly outdated | Facilities in the demo |
| Blood stock, holds, requests | Generated by `seed-demo` | n/a | Synthetic, labelled | No open real-time stock data exists (e-RaktKosh has no public API) |
| Donors | Generated by `seed-demo` | n/a | Synthetic, labelled | Real donor data is personal health data: never used |

## Looked at and not used
- National Health Portal "Blood Bank Directory" on AIKOSH / data.gov.in (GODL): exists, but no working direct download
  was found; its 2017 content reaches us through the OpenStreetMap import anyway.
- e-RaktKosh live stock: no public API or open-data licence; scraping it would not be appropriate.
- Aggregate counts (licensed blood centres per state, Dataful): useful for slides only.
